Summary — Assessment of Students
Assessment is the process of making a judgment about student learning. The word comes from Latin assidere meaning "to sit beside".
Types: Teacher Observation (most common in PE), Homework, Peer Observation, Self-Assessment.
Factors influencing assessment: Physical (age, health, disabilities), School (safety, absenteeism), Teacher (teaching style, familiarity with test).
Tools: Checklists (systematic data collection), Rubrics (criteria-based scoring — 3-point: A/B/C or 5-point: A/B/C/D/E).
Benchmark: Standard norms created from data across schools/states for the same age group, separate for boys & girls.
Evaluation types: Formative (ongoing, monitors progress), Summative (end of course, judges mastery), Diagnostic (identifies specific problems).
Qualitative assessment: Based on quality/form of skill using rubrics (e.g. running form). Quantitative: Based on measurable scores compared to norms (e.g. fitness tests).
Fitness tests: Aerobic (600m run, Beep test), Anaerobic (30m sprint), Flexibility (Sit & Reach), Abdominal (Curl-ups), Lower body power (Standing Broad Jump), Upper body (Medicine Ball Throw).
Assessment steps: Pre (practice, schedule, consent, ground marking, data collection) → During (safety check, warm-up, record scores) → Post (upload data, generate reports, PTM).
Summary — Communication Skills
Communication is a two-way process of exchanging information between sender and receiver using language, symbols, signs or behaviour.
4 key skills (LSRW): Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing.
Communication cycle: Sender → Encoding → Message → Channel → Receiver → Decoding → Feedback.
Effective message must be: Clear (exact meaning), Concise (brief, to the point), Accurate (factual, verifiable).
Active Listening factors: Eye contact, Gestures, Avoiding distractions, Giving feedback.
5 Stages of Active Listening: Receiving → Understanding → Remembering → Evaluating → Responding.
RESPECT: Remove distractions, Eye contact, Show listening, Pay attention, Empathise, Clarify doubts, Tune to speaker's timing.
Barriers to listening: Being pre-occupied, Noise/visual distractions, Past experiences/mindset, Personal factors.
Parts of Speech: Nouns, Pronouns, Adjectives, Verbs, Adverbs + Supporting: Articles, Conjunctions, Prepositions, Interjections.
MINTS (capitalisation): Months, I, Names, Titles, Starting letter of sentences.
Sentence types: Declarative (fact, full stop), Interrogative (question, ?), Exclamatory (emotion, !), Imperative (command/request).
Voice: Active (subject does action) vs Passive (subject receives action). Objects: Direct (what?) vs Indirect (to whom?).
