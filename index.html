Quick Revision Summary for Tomorrow's Exam

High-yield topics extracted directly from Unit 1 & Communication Skills.

Unit 1: Assessment of Students

Physical Activity Trainer (XII)

1. Body Mass Index (BMI) & Growth Monitoring

Formula: BMI = Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]². Used to evaluate body composition and assess underweight, normal, or obese parameters among school students.

2. Kraus-Weber Test & Posture Assessment

Tests minimum muscular strength of trunk and hip flexors. Measures abdominal strength, lower back flexibility, and identifies postural deformities like Lordosis, Kyphosis, and Flat Foot.

3. Motor Fitness & Physical Performance Tests

Includes 50m Standing Start (Speed), 600m Run/Walk (Endurance), Sit & Reach (Flexibility), Shuttle Run (Agility), and Partial Curl-up (Abdominal Strength).

4. Record Keeping & Fitness Progress Cards

Systematic recording of anthropometric measurements, fitness scores, and periodic health evaluation reports for tracking student growth.

Module 1: Communication Skills

Employability Skills (XII)

1. Methods of Communication

Verbal (Oral & Written), Non-Verbal (Body language, gestures, eye contact, posture), and Visual (Charts, graphs, diagrams, signage).

2. The 7 Cs of Effective Communication

Clear, Concise, Concrete, Correct, Coherent, Complete, and Courteous. Ensures clear message delivery without misinterpretation.

3. Barriers to Communication

Environmental/Physical, Language/Semantic, Cultural, Psychological (Anxiety, prejudice), and Organizational structure barriers.

4. Active Listening & Feedback Mechanisms

Giving constructive feedback (Specific, timely, polite), overcoming listening barriers, and maintaining active eye contact during professional dialogue.
